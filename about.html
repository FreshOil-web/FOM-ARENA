About Fresh Oil Ministries

Knowing Christ • Living His Word • Revealing His Glory

Who We Are

Fresh Oil Ministries (F.O.M.) is a Bible-believing church committed to revealing Jesus Christ through sound biblical teaching, passionate worship, fervent prayer, and compassionate service. We are known as The Liberation House, Where Jesus Is Revealed, where lives are transformed by the power of God and people experience freedom, hope, and restoration through Jesus Christ.

Our Foundation

Our foundation is built upon the unchanging Word of God and the finished work of Jesus Christ. We believe that every believer is called to grow spiritually, walk in holiness, and demonstrate the love and power of God in everyday life.

At Fresh Oil Ministries, everything we do is centred on glorifying Christ, empowering believers, and advancing the Kingdom of God.

Our Mission

To reveal Jesus Christ by preaching the Gospel, making disciples, raising spiritually mature believers, restoring broken lives through prayer and the Word of God, and equipping people to impact their communities for the glory of God.

Our Vision

To be a Christ-centred ministry that transforms lives, builds strong families, develops leaders, and reaches nations with the love and power of Jesus Christ.

Our Core Values

Christ-Centred

Jesus Christ is the foundation of everything we do.

Prayer

We believe prayer changes lives and opens heaven.

Holiness

Living a life that honours God in word and deed.

Love

Serving God by loving and caring for people.

Excellence

Giving our very best in every area of ministry.

Integrity

Walking honestly, faithfully and with godly character.

You Are Always Welcome

Whether you are seeking God, looking for a church family, or simply visiting, there is a place for you at Fresh Oil Ministries. Come and experience the presence of God with us.
