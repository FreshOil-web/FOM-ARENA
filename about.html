About Fresh Oil Ministries

Knowing Christ • Living His Word • Revealing His Glory

Who We Are

Fresh Oil Ministries (F.O.M.) is a Bible-believing church committed to revealing Jesus Christ through sound biblical teaching, passionate worship, fervent prayer, and compassionate service. We are known as The Liberation House, Where Jesus Is Revealed, where lives are transformed by the power of God and people experience freedom, hope, and restoration through Jesus Christ.

Our Foundation

Our foundation is built upon the unchanging Word of God and the finished work of Jesus Christ. We believe that every believer is called to grow spiritually, walk in holiness, and demonstrate the love and power of God in everyday life.

At Fresh Oil Ministries, everything we do is centred on glorifying Christ, empowering believers, and advancing the Kingdom of God.

Our Mission

“To proclaim Christ, teach Christ, and form Christ in people— so that every believer matures into the full measure of Christ, resulting in a Christified Generation that impacts lives, communities, and nations.” |Colossians 1:28:| “Whom we preach, warning every man, and teaching every man in all wisdom; that we may present every man perfect in Christ Jesus.”

Our Vision

“To see the Body of Christ become a truly Christified people— walking in unity beyond tribal, social, denominational, and gender barriers; abandoning self-centered living to reflect the nature, character, and authority of Christ; and standing together as one Body to establish and advance the Kingdom of God on the earth.”

Our Principles

The Kingship of Christ

Jesus Christ is not only Savior, Healer, Deliverer, Provider, Teacher, and Comforter. Above all, He is King of kings and Lord of lords, and His Lordship governs everything we do.

The Body Reflects the Head

The Church is called to be like Christ—not only in love and holiness, but also in authority, power, and dominion. Christ is returning for a spotless, glorious Bride who resembles Him.

Kingdom-Centered Gospel

The Gospel is not about self-advancement; it is about the King and His Kingdom.

The Word Of God as Final Authority

Scripture is our: • Manufacturer’s Handbook • General’s Battle Plan • Bridegroom’s Love Letter

It is the final standard for belief, conduct, and ministry.

Wholehearted Commitment

Lukewarm Christianity is ineffective, unattractive, and spiritually destructive.

We pursue wholehearted devotion

Repentance and Transformation

Continuous repentance produces continuous transformation in heart, mind, and action

Holiness and Grace

Holiness is God’s command; grace is God’s enabling power to live victoriously.

Prayer as Foundation

Prayer is the underpinning of all ministry, keeping us dependent on God rather than human strength.

Lifestyle Worship

Worship is not limited to song, but expressed in life, obedience, and service.

Practical and Relational Faith

Faith must be lived out practically and grounded in an intimate relationship with God.

Biblical Balance

We pursue the fullness of God’s truth without compromise, neglect, or exaggeration.
