Our Ministries

Serving God • Serving People • Growing Together

Ministry Departments

At Fresh Oil Ministries, everyone has a place to grow, serve, and discover their God-given purpose. Our ministries are designed to nurture faith, build relationships, and equip believers to impact the Kingdom of God.

🙏 Intercession Ministry

Standing in the gap through prayer and intercession, believing God for healing, breakthrough, restoration, and revival.

🎵 Praise & Worship Ministry

Leading the congregation into God's presence through praise, worship, and musical excellence.

Outreach

They are dedicated to sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ with the community and demonstrating God's love through evangelism, compassion, and service.

👨 Men's Ministry

Building godly men who lead their families, churches, workplaces, and communities with integrity.

👩 Women's Ministry

Encouraging women to grow spiritually, support one another, and fulfill God's purpose for their lives.

🧒 Children's Ministry

Teaching children the love of Jesus in a joyful, safe, and engaging environment.

🔥 Youth Ministry

Empowering young people to boldly follow Christ and become leaders in their generation.

🌍 Evangelism

Sharing the Gospel through community outreach, missions, and acts of compassion.

🎥 Media Ministry

Using technology, photography, video, and social media to spread the Gospel beyond the church walls.

Hospitality Ministry

Create a warm, welcoming, and Christ-centered environment for everyone who enters the church

Testimony Ministry

Encourage believers by sharing the life-changing works of God.

Elders Ministry

Provides spiritual leadership, guidance, and pastoral care within the church
