THERE IS A PLACE FOR YOU

Discover Where You Belong

At Fresh Oil Ministries, we believe that every believer has been given unique gifts, talents and a purpose. Our ministries provide opportunities to grow spiritually, build meaningful relationships and use those gifts to serve God and impact lives.

01

Prayer Ministry

Standing in the gap through prayer and intercession, believing God for healing, breakthrough, restoration and revival.

02

Worship Ministry

Leading the congregation into God's presence through praise, worship and musical excellence.

03

Bible Study

Growing deeper in God's Word through teaching, discussion, discipleship and spiritual development.

04

Men's Ministry

Building godly men who lead their families, churches, workplaces and communities with faith, integrity and purpose.

05

Women's Ministry

Encouraging women to grow spiritually, support one another and fulfil God's purpose for their lives.

06

Children's Ministry

Teaching children about the love of Jesus in a joyful, safe and engaging environment where their faith can grow.

07

Youth Ministry

Empowering young people to boldly follow Christ, discover their purpose and become leaders in their generation.

08

Evangelism & Outreach

Sharing the Gospel through evangelism, community outreach, missions and practical acts of compassion.

09

Media Ministry

Using technology, photography, video and social media to communicate the Gospel beyond the walls of the church.
